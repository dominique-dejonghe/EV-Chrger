Quick Start Guide - Learn in 5 minutes

Open Calculator (New Tab)

👆 Click to open the calculator, then follow the steps below

1

Choose Your Vehicle

Search from 138+ electric vehicles. Type "Tesla", "MG", or any brand.

💡 Tip: Click the ⭐ star to save favorites (Premium)
2

Battery Charge Range

Set start (20%) and target (80%) battery levels.

⚡ Fast: 20-80% charges MUCH faster than 80-100%
3

Charging Power

Set charger power (1-350 kW). The app limits to your vehicle's max.

🔌 Common: Home 7kW, Fast 50kW, Supercharger 150kW+
4

Charging Time (Optional)

How much time do you have? Calculate range gained in X minutes.

⏱️ Example: 30 min lunch break = ~200km range
5

Electricity Price

Set your cost per kWh (default €0.30).

💰 Range: Home €0.10 - Fast charger €0.70
6

Calculate!

Click the button to see results: speed (km/h), time, cost.

📊 Results: Charging speed, effective power, total cost

Optimal Fast Charging

Road Trip Strategy

Stop for 15-20 minutes to charge 20-70% instead of waiting 45 min for 100%. You'll save time!
